## What is DisFlow?

DisFlow is a visual builder for Discord bots. Instead of writing code, you connect blocks on a
canvas:

- **Give new members a role** automatically when they join.
- **Reply to messages** and keywords automatically.
- **Create slash commands** like `/ping` or `/help`.
- **Moderate your server** with role management, kicks, bans and timeouts.
- **Send announcements** with embeds, buttons and files.

When you are happy with your bot, export it and run it on your computer or a server.

## How it works

1. **Add your bot** to DisFlow using its Discord token.
2. **Build its behaviour** by connecting blocks in the editor.
3. **Export your bot** and follow the short setup steps to get it online.

No coding experience is needed. If you can connect the dots, you can build a bot.
